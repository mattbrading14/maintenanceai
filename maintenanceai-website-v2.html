The platform

The maintenance side of property management, finally in one place.

Most property managers are not short on effort. They are stuck coordinating the same messy work across inboxes, texts, vendor calls, and spreadsheets. MaintenanceAI centralizes the flow.

Work Orders

Turn tenant texts, emails, or screenshots into structured work orders with category, priority, vendor match, and approval status.

Bid Requests

Send one scope to multiple contractors, track every response, and generate an AI comparison across price, timeline, and value.

Preventive Maintenance

Schedule recurring tasks once. MaintenanceAI tracks due dates, sends reminders, logs completions, and advances the next cycle.

Owner Reports

Maintenance history, completed PM tasks, bid outcomes, vendor activity, and costs roll into clean owner-facing reports.

Core workflows

Built around the work that actually eats your week.

Who it is for

For managers who need maintenance under control, not another giant system.

MaintenanceAI is a fit if...

  • ✓You manage residential, commercial, or mixed portfolios.
  • ✓Your tenants already text, call, or email maintenance issues.
  • ✓You use trusted local vendors and want better coordination.
  • ✓You need clean owner reports without manual write-ups.
  • ✓You want preventive maintenance to stop falling through the cracks.

It is not trying to be...

  • —A replacement for your accounting, rent collection, or leasing system.
  • —A tenant portal that forces renters into a new habit.
  • —A marketplace that pushes you away from your own vendors.
  • —An enterprise platform built for thousands of units and long implementation cycles.
