Updated services

The building workflows that usually live in texts, inboxes, and spreadsheets.

MaintenanceAI now covers the day-to-day property work that small commercial, residential, mixed-use, and HOA managers asked for: requests, bids, recurring maintenance, owner reports, leasing leads, turnovers, and room reservations.

Work Orders

Read tenant emails, texts, voicemail transcripts, and screenshots, then draft a structured work order with priority, category, vendor match, approval status, and notes.

Bid Requests

Create one scope, invite multiple vendors, track responses, upload bids, and compare price, exclusions, timeline, warranty, and overall value side by side.

Preventive Maintenance

Schedule recurring tasks, track due dates, log completion history, carry estimated costs, and keep the next cycle from getting lost.

Owner Reports

Generate date-range reports with work order logs, notes, completed totals, estimated totals, preventive maintenance, bids, and booking activity.

Prospective Tenant Database

Save names, emails, phone numbers, and flexible notes for office or apartment prospects, then email everyone when a matching space becomes available.

Office & Apartment Turnovers

Select the vacant space, choose the needed work, attach photos, auto-match vendors by scope, and send clean turnover instructions for approval.

Room Bookings

Read room rental requests from screenshots or messages, capture renter details, rooms, dates, times, kitchen use, pricing, calendar status, and booking reports.

Who it is for

For managers who need maintenance under control, not another giant system.

MaintenanceAI is a fit if...

  • ✓You manage residential, commercial, or mixed portfolios.
  • ✓Your tenants, prospects, vendors, or room renters already reach out by text, email, calls, or screenshots.
  • ✓You use trusted local vendors and want better coordination.
  • ✓You need clean owner reports without manual write-ups.
  • ✓You want preventive maintenance, turnovers, bookings, and follow-up to stop falling through the cracks.

It is not trying to be...

  • —A replacement for your accounting, rent collection, or leasing system.
  • —A tenant portal that forces renters into a new habit.
  • —A marketplace that pushes you away from your own vendors.
  • —An enterprise platform built for thousands of units and long implementation cycles.
