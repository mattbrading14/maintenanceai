Updated services

The building workflows that usually live in texts, inboxes, and spreadsheets.

Start with the work your building needs: requests, bids, recurring maintenance, owner reports, office leads, office turnovers, and room reservations. The current beta grew out of hands-on management of a small office building.

Work Orders

Upload a screenshot of a tenant email or text, or log a call. Review the issue, location, priority, vendor, and notes before preparing the next step.

Bid Requests

Create one scope, invite multiple vendors, track responses, upload bids, and compare price, exclusions, timeline, warranty, and overall value side by side.

Preventive Maintenance

In the latest beta preview, record the actual service date and optional time—even when you are logging it later. Link the existing work order, correct completion details, and review the next due date.

Owner Reports

In the latest beta preview, generate maintenance reports by date range, with completed work counted by service date, status notes, estimated costs, and the next preventive maintenance dates. Room rental reports are available separately.

Office Leads

Keep office prospects and their preferences together, then prepare an availability email for your review when a space opens up.

Office Turnovers

Choose an office, select painting, carpet, cleaning, locks, blinds, or other work, add photos, and review vendor assignments and email drafts. Keep the turnover scope together through completion.

Room Bookings

Review room rental requests, renter details, dates, times, kitchen use, pricing, and booking reports. Calendar synchronization depends on the property’s configured connection.

Who it is for

For managers who need maintenance under control, not another giant system.

MaintenanceAI is a fit if...

  • ✓You manage residential, commercial, or mixed portfolios.
  • ✓Your tenants, prospects, vendors, or room renters already reach out by text, email, calls, or screenshots.
  • ✓You use trusted local vendors and want better coordination.
  • ✓You need clean owner reports without manual write-ups.
  • ✓You want preventive maintenance, turnovers, bookings, and follow-up to stop falling through the cracks.

  • —A replacement for your accounting, rent collection, or leasing system.
  • —A tenant portal that forces renters into a new habit.

  • —An enterprise platform built for thousands of units and long implementation cycles.

Common questions

How MaintenanceAI fits your building.

What is MaintenanceAI?

MaintenanceAI is a beta web app for organizing building maintenance. It brings together work orders, vendors, preventive maintenance, office turnover tasks, room bookings, and maintenance reports.

Do tenants need to install an app?

No. Tenants can keep contacting the manager by text, email, or phone. The manager uploads a screenshot or enters the request in MaintenanceAI and reviews the details.

Can I record maintenance after it has already happened?

Yes. Record the actual completion date, add an optional time and notes, link the existing work order, and confirm the next due date. Regenerate the owner report to include the correction.

Can I share a demo without sharing tenant information?

Yes. The public sample-building demo uses fictional records in an isolated browser frame. It has no connection to the live property database, and changes reset when the page reloads.

Does it automatically send messages or replace accounting software?

Vendor workflows prepare email or text drafts for manager review. It is not a full accounting system. Booked rental amounts and estimated maintenance costs should not be treated as collected revenue or paid expenses.

Can a group of buildings report to one central team?

That is a proposed pilot capability. It would require property-specific permissions, consistent data definitions, and a central reporting dashboard. It is not an existing statewide deployment or an endorsement by any organization.
