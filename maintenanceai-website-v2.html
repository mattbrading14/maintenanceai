How it works

Works the way
you already work

Your tenants keep texting and emailing you. You keep using your vendors. MaintenanceAI just makes sure every request gets captured, prioritized, and dispatched — without anything slipping through.

01
📱

Tenant texts, calls, or emails you

No app download. No portal. No new habit for your tenant. They reach out exactly the way they always have.

02
📸

Screenshot it — AI does the rest

Screenshot the text, email, or voicemail transcript. AI reads it, categorizes the issue, sets priority, and recommends your vendor by trade.

03
✅

You approve with one tap

Review the structured work order and approve or deny. Nothing goes to a vendor without your sign-off. You stay in control.

04
🔧

Your vendor gets the job

The vendor you already trust gets the details instantly by email. No phone tag, no copy-paste, no dropped requests.

05
📅

Preventive maintenance runs itself

HVAC filters, fire extinguishers, annual inspections — scheduled once, dispatched automatically when due. Nothing slips.

06
📊

Owner reports write themselves

AI generates a professional monthly maintenance summary for property owners — every work order logged, no manual write-up needed.
