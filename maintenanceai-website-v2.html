Common questions

Know what you’re trying.

Where can I use screenshot reading?

The beta includes screenshot-reading flows for maintenance requests, room reservations, office leads, office turnovers, and past maintenance emails. Review extracted information before using it. Try the public screenshot demo for browser-based text reading and draft review. Request a walkthrough to see the app’s full AI extraction workflows.

What can I try today?

Start by uploading a redacted screenshot or selecting a fictional sample, then read the text and review a demo draft. You can also expand the fictional sample building to explore work orders, maintenance dates, turnovers, and an owner report.

What happens when a maintenance date passes?

The current beta has in-app follow-up choices to record completion, leave work overdue, or postpone the prompt. Recording completion asks for the actual service date. These are in-app prompts, not text or push notifications.

Can I upload service reports and invoices?

Private document uploads are not yet enabled. Keep those files in your organization’s approved records location. Document reading and storage are part of the development work, not a capability to rely on in the public demo.

Can a central team see multiple properties?

A shared portfolio dashboard is planned. It requires property-specific permissions and consistent reporting definitions before a pilot can support central access. The demo’s portfolio tab is explicitly a concept.

Does a booked amount mean payment was received?

No. Bookings, estimates, invoices, and confirmed payments are different records. MaintenanceAI is not a replacement for accounting or payment reconciliation.

How do we discuss fit and pricing?

Request a walkthrough and describe your building’s needs. Scope, availability, and pricing should be confirmed before any commitment; a request does not start a subscription.
