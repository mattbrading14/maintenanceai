Office turnovers

A clear plan from move-out to ready for the next tenant.

An office turnover checklist is a record of the condition, work, responsibility, and final inspection needed before a space is ready for a new occupant. Keep one list for the office, then assign each task to a vendor or team member.

Download the checklist

What belongs on the checklist?

StageRecord or checkDone when
WalkthroughOffice number, move-out date, photos, keys returned, visible damage, and proposed ready date.Condition and scope are documented.
Scope & approvalsPainting, carpet cleaning or replacement, blinds, bulbs, locks, HVAC and plumbing issues. Identify who approves costs.Vendors and the approved scope are clear.
ScheduleSequence dusty repairs and painting before final cleaning. Record vendor dates and access arrangements.Every open task has an owner and next step.
Complete workActual completion date, cost documentation, photos, remaining defects, and any change to scope.Work is checked, not merely scheduled.
Ready for occupancyFinal walkthrough, clean condition, working fixtures, keys, and any required professional sign-offs.The manager confirms the ready date.

How do I prevent missed tasks?

Use a status for every item: not started, scheduled, in progress, or completed. A vendor appointment is not proof of completion. Record the actual service date and check the result before closing the item.

How does MaintenanceAI help?

The office turnover workflow groups the space, photos, selected work, and vendor assignments. Managers review the vendor email drafts before sending. Related work stays searchable in the app.
